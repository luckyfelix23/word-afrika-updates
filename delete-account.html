Delete your Word Afrika account and data

Word Afrika is made by Retrohorizon Games. The developer is Lucky Aikorogie. You can delete your account and data at any time.

Delete in the game

  1. Connect to the internet and open Word Afrika.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Choose Delete my data.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

Delete by email

If you can no longer open the game, email luckyfelix202@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Word Afrika data" and your username. We may ask you to confirm the account is yours. We will delete your data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your game account and anonymous player ID
  • Your reserved username
  • Your leaderboard results and game statistics
  • Your online challenges
  • Your feedback messages
  • Your Google save, including your Google email, account ID, country, state, coins and progress
  • Your profile, coins, levels and settings on your device, when you delete in the game

What is kept

We do not keep any of this data after deletion. Deleted data cannot be restored. Uninstalling the game does not delete your online data, so please use one of the options above.
