Word Afrika Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy explains how the Word Afrika game, made by Retrohorizon Games, handles your information.

Information you choose to add

You may add a username, email address, profile photo, country and state. The game also saves your coins, score, levels, game statistics and settings. If you choose your own background music, the game keeps a copy of that file for playback.

Where it is stored

Your profile email, photo and music stay on your device. Your country, state, coins and game progress also stay on your device unless you choose Google save. We do not sell your information.

Online leaderboard

If you create a profile, your username is reserved in Google Firebase with your anonymous player ID so that no one else can use it. Your username, score, levels, play time, games played and wins are stored in Google Firebase with an anonymous player ID. Your username and results are shown to other players on the leaderboard. Do not use your full name or private information as your username.

Google account save

You can choose Continue with Google to save your progress and restore it on another phone. Firebase then stores your Google email and account ID, plus your username, country, state, coins, levels and game statistics. Only your signed-in account can read this saved progress.

Online challenges

When you create or accept an online challenge, your username, online games played, online wins, language, difficulty and match progress are stored in Google Firebase and shown to other players. Your match result is also shown to the other player. Challenges are temporary, and the game removes them when you use Delete my data.

Notifications and feedback

The game checks Firebase for messages from Retrohorizon Games and for open challenges. Notifications are saved on your device, and you can empty your message box at any time. If you send feedback, your message, username, anonymous player ID and game version are stored in Firebase so we can read it. Do not include private information in your feedback.

Offline multiplayer

When you start an offline game, your username is sent directly to the other player's device over the hotspot connection so they can see who they are playing.

Update check

When the game opens, it requests a small file from GitHub to check for a new version. GitHub receives normal connection details, such as your IP address, under its own privacy terms. We do not use this request to identify you.

Device access

The game opens your photo or file picker only when you choose to add a profile photo or your own music. It uses network access for the update check, offline multiplayer, online challenges, notifications, feedback and the online leaderboard.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your online leaderboard information, reserved username and feedback are kept until you delete them. To permanently delete your device information, leaderboard results, reserved username, challenges, feedback, Google save and game account, open Settings, choose Delete my data, and type DELETE while connected to the internet. We cannot restore deleted data. Uninstalling removes information from your device, but it does not remove your online leaderboard results. If you can no longer use the game, email us at the address below with your username and we will help delete your results.

Security

Information on your device is kept in the game's private storage. Online information is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules. Keep your device protected with a screen lock.

Children

Word Afrika is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page if the game changes how it handles information, including before adding payments or other new services.
